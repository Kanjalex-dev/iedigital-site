Forge Yourself

Strength-training programme and progress tracking. Forge builds your programme around your equipment, your level and your training days, then adjusts the load for each exercise session after session, based on what you log.

At a glance

iPhone, iOS 17 or later. In preparation, not yet available on the App Store.
Account

Data
Stored on your iPhone. Export a backup at any time.
Plans
Today’s log is free; programme, automatic progression and history with a subscription.
