Ferralis

Privacy policy

This is an English translation provided for convenience. The French version is the reference text.

Ferralis, iOS app, Apple Watch app and web app · version of 25 October 2026 · see also the terms of use and the purchase terms

In short: we do not receive any of your training or health data. There is no account, no Ferralis server, no advertising, no trackers and no third-party tool built into the app. What you enter is processed by the app on your device. On iPhone, if an iCloud account is signed in, a copy of your programme and your strength-training sessions goes to your iCloud space, which we cannot access. Your body measurements, everything that comes from the Health app (including, from version 1.6, your cardio sessions, your heart rate and your routes), your meal log and its photos are never copied by Ferralis, neither to iCloud nor anywhere else. If you agree, Ferralis saves certain sessions to the Health app, on your device: nothing reaches us.

What the app processes, and where

  • Your programme, sessions, loads and notes: stored in the app’s storage, on your device.
  • Your weight, height, age and body measurements: same place. They are used to draw your progress chart and, from age 18, to calculate general nutrition guidelines. They are not shared with anyone, including us.
  • In the web app, everything is kept in your browser’s storage. Clearing the site’s data erases it. The web app does not sync with iCloud.

We do not have access to this data: we cannot read, correct or delete it on your behalf. You manage it yourself in the app; Profile › Data › Delete this profile erases the profile and its entire history, on the device and, while sync is active, in your iCloud copy. Deleting the app erases its data from the device, but not your iCloud copy (see below).

Your consent, and why we explain it to you anyway

What the app processes on your device, it processes at your request and on your own behalf: we do not have access to it. Even so, we designed Ferralis to process only what is needed for what you ask of it, and we inform you here as if we were responsible for it. For your health data (body measurements, data read from the Health app, meal log), Ferralis only uses it with your explicit consent: first the Ferralis explanation screen, then the iOS permission window. You can withdraw this consent at any time, with no effect on the rest of the app; withdrawing it does not affect what was done before.

iCloud sync (iPhone)

Since version 1.2.1, as soon as an iCloud account is signed in on your iPhone, Ferralis copies your profiles — programme, sessions, loads, notes, medals and settings — to the private database that your iCloud account reserves for the app. This is what lets you find them again on another iPhone. This applies to all users of the iPhone app, with or without a subscription. Sync happens on its own: when the app launches, when you return to it, at the end of a session and when it goes into the background.

  • What never goes there: your weight, height, age and body measurements, whether they come from Ferralis or from the Health app; other data read from the Health app, including, from version 1.6, your cardio sessions and their heart rate, active energy and routes; your meal log and its photos; your nutrition data; your Health-related choices; the areas you have chosen to go easy on; the estimated calories of your sessions; the session in progress. This data stays on the iPhone where you entered it.
  • Who has access: you, through your Apple Account. This copy is stored by Apple, within your iCloud storage, under the iCloud terms you have accepted. It does not pass through any IE DIGITAL server, and we cannot view it.
  • Without an iCloud account signed in on the iPhone, nothing is sent: your data stays on the iPhone.
  • Not to be confused with your iPhone backup (in iCloud or on a computer), which is managed by iOS, not by Ferralis: as with any app, it contains the Ferralis data present on the device, except meal log photos.
  • To stop: turn off Ferralis in Settings › your name › iCloud. The copy already made stays in your iCloud until you erase it.
  • To erase your iCloud copy: delete your profiles in Ferralis while sync is active, or erase Ferralis’s data in Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.

The meal log (iPhone, optional)

The meal log only opens with your explicit consent. For each meal, it keeps its composition, a plate size and, if you wish, a photo. The photo is taken with the iPhone camera, analysed on your iPhone to suggest a composition that you confirm yourself, reduced in size, then deleted after 7 days. The log and its photos stay on your iPhone: Ferralis does not copy them to iCloud or to us, and the photos are excluded from iPhone backups. The relevance indicator is off by default; when turned on, it is calculated on the iPhone. Erase all deletes the meals and photos; withdrawing your consent erases the entire log.

Your meals eaten out (iPhone, subscription)

When you say where you are eating (fast food, delivery, family meal…), Ferralis keeps this choice on your iPhone so it can make you the same suggestion next time, at the same point in the week. This calculation is done on the iPhone, without artificial intelligence. This choice is part of your nutrition data: it goes neither to iCloud nor to us. You can turn off this memory and erase what has been remembered with Nutrition › Summary › Method › Your settings › Take my habits into account (on by default; when off, nothing more is remembered). You can also erase it with Erase all in the meal log, or by deleting the profile.

Importing a file (iPhone, optional)

Profile › Data › Import from another app (or, when creating a profile, the link in the “Your loads” chapter) lets you choose a CSV file of sessions exported by another tracking app. The file is read once, on your iPhone, to extract your sessions and loads: it is not sent, copied or kept by Ferralis. The sessions retained join your history, in the same place as those you do in Ferralis, and follow the same rules (backup, iCloud sync, deletion). Nothing is written to the Health app from an import.

The Health app and Apple Watch (optional)

Everything to do with the Health app is optional. Ferralis first explains, in the app, what it will read or write and why; then iOS lets you choose item by item. You can refuse all or part of it: the rest of the app works normally. Each consent can be withdrawn at any time (see below).

On the iPhone: weight, height, steps. If you turn on Profile › Data › Health (the setting applies to the open profile: on a shared iPhone, weigh-ins from Health only go to the profile that turned it on), Ferralis reads from the Health app your weight and height, to fill in your progress chart and guidelines without entering them twice, and your step count, to show your daily average in the Progress tab.

On the iPhone: your cardio sessions (from version 1.6). If you turn on cardio session import (off by default), Ferralis reads from the Health app the workouts other than strength training that you have recorded there (running, cycling, rowing, swimming, intervals…), with the following data for each one, where available:

  • the activity type, start, end and duration;
  • the distance and pace;
  • the heart rate: average, highest value during the session, and curve during the session;
  • the active energy, as estimated by Apple;
  • the session’s route (GPS track), only if you also allow it: iOS asks for this consent separately, and without it Ferralis shows a generic image instead of the track.

This data is used for three things, and nothing else: displaying your cardio sessions, counting your minutes and kilometres per week, and organising your strength-training programme around your cardio days. For this last point, Ferralis relies on the type and duration of the session, never on your heart rate. Heart rate and energy are displayed as the Health app provides them, without interpretation, without comparison to a norm and without alerts.

The base map (from version 1.6, off by default). By default, the route is displayed on a plain background: nothing leaves your iPhone. If you choose to display the base map, the iPhone requests the map images from Apple’s servers: the area displayed and your IP address are then sent to Apple, which processes them on its own behalf under its privacy policy. This area may reveal where your outings start and finish. We receive nothing. You can switch back to the plain background at any time in the app’s settings.

What Ferralis writes to Health.

  • On the Apple Watch. If you give your separate, optional consent (Profile › Data › Health › Apple Watch), a session you start on your watch is saved to Health as a “strength training” workout: its start, end and duration, and the active energy and heart rate measured by the watch. During the session, the watch reads your heart rate and active energy to display them, without interpreting them. Without this consent, the watch still works: it tells you when your rest is over with a notification.
  • Guided interval sessions (from version 1.6). If you allow it, an interval session done with Ferralis is saved to Health. Done on the Apple Watch: its type, start, end and duration, and the active energy and heart rate measured by the watch. Done on the iPhone alone: its type, start, end and duration, without energy or heart rate, which Ferralis does not calculate.
  • Nothing else is written to Health. Up to version 1.5, the iPhone writes nothing to Health.

What goes nowhere. Nothing read from or written to Health is sent to us: there is no Ferralis server. This data, including your cardio sessions, your heart rate, your active energy and your routes, goes neither to iCloud nor to the file backup; the watch only sends the iPhone the identifier of the saved session. It is never used for advertising or marketing, is not used for any profiling, and is neither sold nor shared with a third party.

Withdrawing your consent. In Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Ferralis, on the iPhone, item by item. In Ferralis, turning off Profile › Data › Health, cardio session import or Profile › Data › Health › Apple Watch stops the corresponding reading or saving. Withdrawing consent does not delete what is already in Health; your cardio sessions simply stop appearing in Ferralis and your programme no longer takes your cardio days into account.

Deleting sessions. Sessions saved by Ferralis are deleted in the Health app (Browse › Activity › Workouts). Deleting a Ferralis profile or the app does not erase what is in Health: Health is managed by Apple, on your device, and that is where you delete it.

File backup

Profile › Data › Back up this profile creates a file that you can keep wherever you like (Files, iCloud Drive, email…). It contains your profile, including the body measurements you entered, but nothing that comes from Health, and not your meal log. Ferralis does not send it anywhere: you choose its destination, and the service you choose then applies its own rules. This file also lets you retrieve your data in a readable format and take it elsewhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase go entirely through Apple, which collects payment and processes this data on its own behalf. We do not receive your name, your address or your payment details. The app checks the receipt signed by Apple on your device to know which features to unlock. Management and cancellation are done in your Apple Account settings, or from Ferralis with Manage Subscription, which opens Apple’s window. See the purchase terms.

Review requests

From time to time, after a session, Ferralis may display the App Store review prompt. This is an Apple prompt: iOS decides whether it appears and limits how often, and what you answer goes to Apple, not to us. To choose the moment, the app only uses data that stays on your iPhone (number of sessions, personal records, date of the last renewal). If you post a review, Apple displays it on the App Store under your nickname, according to its own rules; we may reply publicly.

Notifications

The rest timer can send you notifications and appear on the Lock Screen, if you allow it. On the Apple Watch, the end of your rest can also come as a notification. They are created by the app on your iPhone or your watch; no server sends them.

Statistics

Ferralis does not include any audience measurement tool. Apple provides us, in its developer portal, with aggregated statistics: installations, crashes, subscriptions. Usage figures and crash reports only come from people who have agreed, in their iPhone settings, to share their analytics data with developers; they do not allow us to identify you. The web app measures nothing.

The web app and this page are hosted by GitHub, Inc. (United States). Like any hosting provider, GitHub may record technical connection data, including your IP address, for the security of its service. We do not have access to it. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you contact us

If you write to us at contact@iedigital.fr, we use your address and the content of your message to reply to you, and only for that purpose. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us or, if you are a customer, the performance of our obligations to you. Only the manager of IE DIGITAL and our email service provider, which hosts it on our behalf, have access to it. We keep these messages for as long as it takes to handle your request, then for no more than three years after our last exchange. Please do not include health data: we do not need it.

How long

  • On your device: until you delete the profile, the site data or the app.
  • In your iCloud (your profiles, if an iCloud account is signed in on the iPhone): until you delete the profile or erase Ferralis’s data in iCloud.
  • Meal log photos: 7 days, on the iPhone.
  • Sessions saved by Ferralis to the Health app (watch and, from version 1.6, interval sessions): until you delete them in Health.
  • Cardio sessions read from the Health app (from version 1.6): they stay in Health, which keeps them according to your own settings; Ferralis keeps no copy of them outside your device.
  • Base map choice (from version 1.6): kept on the iPhone until you change it or delete the app.
  • Your messages: no more than three years after our last exchange.

Minors

Your age is self-declared and is not verified. Under 18, Ferralis does not display calorie or macronutrient guidelines.

Your rights

For data processed on your device or copied to your iCloud, you exercise your rights yourself in the app: view, edit, export (Profile › Data › Back up this profile), delete. For anything that goes through Apple (purchases, iCloud, reviews, base map), you exercise your rights with Apple; what is in the Health app is viewed and deleted in Health. For the messages you have sent us, you can ask us for access, rectification, erasure or restriction, object to their processing, or give us instructions on what should happen to them after your death, at contact@iedigital.fr. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr), or with the data protection authority of your country.

Changes

If the way the app works changes, this page is updated before the relevant version of the app, and the date at the top of the page changes. Passages marked “from version 1.6” describe features that do not exist in earlier versions. Any change that sends new data off your device will be announced to you in the app.
