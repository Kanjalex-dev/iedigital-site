iOS app

Essor

The art of living well for longer, every day. Three small actions a day, a French recipe in the evening, and an index that explains its calculations.

At a glance

Platform
iPhone, iOS 26 or later. For people aged 18 or over only.
Account
None. No Essor server, no ads, no trackers.
Data
Stored and encrypted on the iPhone. Encrypted export and erasure from within the app.
Plans
Actions, cooking, index and blood test reports free; ideas linked to your blood test reports, observations and unlimited dinner ideas with Essor Premium.
Nature
Wellbeing app. It does not replace a doctor’s advice and does not make any diagnosis.
Publisher
IE DIGITAL
