Mobile apps

iOS app development

IE DIGITAL designs, develops and publishes native iOS apps, from the first mock-up to the App Store listing, then maintains them.

What is delivered

Scoping and mock-ups
Target audience, scope of the first version, business model. Mock-ups signed off before the first line of code.
Development
Native iOS, in Swift and SwiftUI. Web apps when the need calls for it.
In-app purchases
Subscriptions and in-app purchases with StoreKit, subscription screens compliant with Apple's rules.
Compliance
App Store Review Guidelines, GDPR, privacy policy and the App Store listing's privacy label.
Publishing
App Store listing, submission, exchanges with Apple's review team.
Maintenance
Updates for new iOS versions, fixes, new features.
