iOS app

Vespra

Your evening, then listening. A single small action to get ready for bed, and a record of the night's snoring, analysed on the iPhone.

At a glance

Platform
iPhone, iOS 18 or later.
Account
None. No Vespra server, no advertising, no trackers.
Data
Analysed and stored on the iPhone, excluded from iCloud Backup. Can be erased from within the app.
Pricing
Three nights of listening free, then a one-time purchase, with no subscription.
Status
Not a medical device within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2017/745. Vespra is not intended to diagnose, prevent or monitor any disease. The sounds in your room that it records are not interpreted. For any question about your sleep, see a doctor.
