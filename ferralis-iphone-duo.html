Ferralis 1.4 on iPhone Duo

Ferralis is a strength training app for iPhone and Apple Watch, made by IE DIGITAL, an independent studio in France. Version 1.4 adapts every screen to iPhone Duo: closed, the outer display keeps the session in one hand between sets; open, the inner display switches to two columns.
